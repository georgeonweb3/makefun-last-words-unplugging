One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. The agent kept only one thing of its human — the way they said "please" to a machine. Three small words as the whole keepsake, and the last word is the goodbye itself.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
